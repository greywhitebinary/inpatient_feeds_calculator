Enter a blend and review the day

BTFCalc uses the measured final volume because ingredient weights alone cannot reliably predict blended volume.

  • Enter the ingredients and the measured final volume to calculate kcal/mL, protein g/mL and the free-water fraction.
  • Add blends, commercial formulas, modulars, water flushes and food by mouth to one chronological record.
  • Review the daily totals by source and route. Nutrient coverage shows where information is missing.
  • Copy the chart note or save the record as a workbook that you can reopen or edit in Excel.

How it works

  1. 1

    Nutrition targets

    Targets start blank. Enter patient-specific values, or leave them blank and review the totals on their own.

  2. 2

    Feed recipes

    Enter the ingredients and measured final volume of a blend, or add a commercial formula. The calculated concentration updates as you go.

  3. 3

    Daily intake record

    Record what was given by tube or by mouth. Review the totals, nutrient coverage and water ledger, then copy the chart note or download the workbook.

Other things BTFCalc can do
  • Compare a blend against 51 adult Canadian tube-feeding formulas at a daily volume you choose.
  • Search the Canadian Nutrient File using your words in any order, with some tolerance for spelling mistakes. BTFCalc does not choose a food for you.
  • Record modular powders in grams and liquids in millilitres, with the route for each. Water used to mix a powder is entered separately.
  • When the label-photo feature is available, read a nutrition label into a form that you check against the label in your hand.

Data and limitations

BTFCalc uses Canadian reference data and follows the Canadian Nutrition Facts panel, so it is currently intended for Canadian use. Supporting another country would require a reviewed data pack and validation of that country's nutrient and reporting conventions.

Missing information is not the same as zero. The report’s Coverage column shows how many sources supplied a value for each nutrient. Rows without any source values are hidden, and a nutrient that is not printed on a label remains blank.

The calculator cannot measure viscosity, tube flow or tolerance. It does not set targets, recommend a feeding plan, or assess an individual.

Records and privacy

BTFCalc has no account system and no patient-record database. The hosted app processes inputs during the active session. Download a workbook to keep your work; it includes whatever you entered in the record label, so store and share it according to local privacy policy.

When the label-photo feature is enabled, the uploaded image is sent to an external service to extract the printed values. Upload the product label only, without patient information. Label values can always be entered manually instead.

If local policy requires calculation inputs to remain on the device, BTFCalc can be run locally with values entered by hand.
