ENCalc

Adult inpatient enteral nutrition calculator, on a Canadian formulary.

Open the calculator →

Free. No account, nothing to install.

Built by Hui Jun Chew, Dietitian

What it does

ENCalc helps you plan a new tube feed, or review one already running, for an adult inpatient. You enter assessment goals and a feeding regimen, and it reports the energy, protein, water and electrolytes that the order delivers, alongside modulars, oral nutritional supplements, IV fluids and propofol.

A feed that is already running can be entered without an assessment. Nothing is calculated backwards from a goal, so the page reports what the order delivers and leaves the goal columns out until goals are entered.

Suggested rates come from the goals, products and other inputs you select. You choose the final rate, the modular order and the hydration plan. It supports clinical judgement rather than substituting for it.

What it calculates

Assessment

  • Energy from five methods shown side by side: a weight-based range in kcal/kg, Mifflin–St Jeor, Harris–Benedict, Penn State 2003b for ventilated adults, and Modified Penn State 2010 for ventilated patients aged 60 or over with a BMI of 30 or over.
  • A choice of weight basis: current weight, Hamwi ideal body weight in SI units, the Devine medication-dosing reference weight, adjusted body weight with an editable factor, or a dry weight you enter yourself.
  • The equations and their worked ranges are shown, so you can see what each input changed before committing to a goal.

The feed

  • Formula comparison across the included Canadian formulary, at a volume you choose.
  • Feed rate for a continuous or cyclic schedule, or for intermittent feeds given either at a rate for a set time or as a set volume.
  • Free water from the formula itself, plus a hydration flush schedule, with a ledger showing where every millilitre came from.
  • Modulars and oral nutritional supplements counted into the same daily totals.
  • Propofol energy taken off the target first, entered as a single rate or as rates that change through the day, so the feed is sized to the remainder.

The record

  • A daily intake table and an EN regimen check.
  • An editable ADIME chart-note draft you can copy out.
  • A case-record workbook you can download and reopen later to restore the assessment, plan inputs and formulary snapshot.

Product data

The included formulary covers 33 enteral formulas, 7 modulars and 20 oral nutritional supplement products, with values taken from manufacturers' Canadian product information. Every row records the document and page it was read from and the date it was checked.

Where a manufacturer has not disclosed a value, the tool shows an em dash rather than a zero, because an undisclosed figure and a declared zero are different facts. Product values stay editable, since local labels and institutional formulary choices differ.

Verify every value against your current local product label and institutional formulary before clinical use. This tool is not a substitute for the label.

Records and privacy

ENCalc has no accounts, no shared workspace and no patient-record database. The hosted app processes your inputs in the active session so it can calculate and render the page, and does not retain them as case records. There is no interface for the site owner to browse patient records.

Case-record workbooks are saved to a location you choose, so their storage and transfer follow your local privacy policy. If policy requires that inputs never leave the clinician's device, the calculator can be run locally instead.
