03

About

My view of healthcare comes from menus, diet orders, records and software fields. I see these operational details as conditions of care. They are invisible when they work. When they fail, people notice too late.

I care about food services and care delivery in healthcare institutions, and how technology and AI become useful in that work, if they become useful at all.

I know enough about healthcare to have opinions, and just enough about technology to be dangerous.
